Flappy Bird needs a browser with canvas support. Press Space, Up Arrow, W or Enter to flap.

Flappy Bird is played with the keyboard or touch. Press Space, Up Arrow, W, Enter, or tap the screen to flap. Fly through the gaps between pipes to score; a gold star is worth 3 points, worth more during a near-miss streak, and a blue bubble is a shield that absorbs one crash. Survive to level 3 to unlock night levels. Press M to mute, P to pause, S for settings, G at the start screen to toggle practice mode, D for the daily challenge, and C after a crash to copy your result. Your best run strokes a ghost on the same course. After a crash, press any flap key again to restart. Focus the game area with Tab; its description includes your score, level, and best score.
